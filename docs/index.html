Privacy Policy for DocScan Pro

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

SmartToolsPro ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DocScan Pro mobile application (the "Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data within the Application, specifically regarding local processing and our integration with Google AdMob for advertising services.

1. Data Collection and Usage

A. Local Document Processing (Non-Collection)

  • Zero Server Uploads: DocScan Pro is engineered to process all document scanning, text recognition (OCR), and file conversions locally on your mobile device.
  • No Document Data Collection: Your scanned documents, images, and texts are stored strictly within your device's isolated storage sandbox or designated public directories. We do not access, collect, upload, or transmit your documents to any external servers.

B. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of this Application, we integrate Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous data to serve relevant ads. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • Approximate location based on your IP address.
  • App interaction data and performance metrics (e.g., crash logs).

For more information on how Google uses your data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

2. Legal Compliance Frameworks

A. European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom:

  • Legal Basis for Processing: Any processing of ad-related data by Google AdMob relies on your explicit consent, which is requested upon the first launch of the Application via a Consent Management Platform (CMP).
  • Your Rights: You have the right to withdraw your consent, access, rectify, or erase your data, and restrict its processing through your device's OS-level privacy settings.

3. United States Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA / COPPA)

  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): We do not sell your personal data. However, the use of third-party advertising cookies or identifiers may be classified as "sharing" or "selling" data under California law. You can opt-out of personalized advertising via your device settings ("Opt out of Ads Personalization").
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): DocScan Pro is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete it from our records.

4. Data Security

The security of your data is paramount. The Application is built using secure deployment practices, including offline local cryptographic key generation (RSA 4096-bit), to ensure the application binary remains tamper-proof, legal, and free from malicious third-party injection.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
